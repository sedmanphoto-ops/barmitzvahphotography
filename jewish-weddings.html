Jewish Wedding Photography

From Bedeken to Hora,
Every Sacred Moment.

Stephen Sedman has photographed Jewish weddings throughout New England for over 30 years, with deep reverence for the traditions that make these celebrations unlike any other.

"A Jewish wedding is not one ceremony — it is a succession of profound moments, each with its own emotional weight. The bedeken. The ketubah signing. Standing beneath the chuppah. The breaking of the glass. The first dance. The hora."

— Stephen Sedman

The Moments We Preserve

🕊

The Bedeken

The veil ceremony. One of the most emotionally charged moments of the day — we never miss it.

📜

Ketubah Signing

The witness signatures, the first quiet moments as a couple before the ceremony begins.

🕍

Chuppah Ceremony

The circling, the blessings, the exchange of rings — all photographed with artistry and discretion.

🥂

Breaking of the Glass

The shout of Mazal Tov, the eruption of joy — a fraction of a second that defines the day.

💃

The Hora

Pure, unfiltered celebration. The most energetic photos of any Jewish wedding come from the hora.

🕯

Candle Lighting

Family, love, generations — all beautifully lit and captured in the glow.
