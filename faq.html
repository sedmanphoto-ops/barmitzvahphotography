Photography & Coverage

The Signature and Heirloom packages include two photographers. The Essential package uses one lead photographer. With two photographers, we can cover the b'nei mitzvah child and their parents simultaneously during key moments — something we highly recommend for complex, multi-location days.

We ask that you provide a "must-have" list of family groupings in advance. During the event, we move through portraits efficiently and warmly — our goal is 45 minutes or less so you can get to your party. Stephen is experienced at organizing large, multigenerational families without chaos.
