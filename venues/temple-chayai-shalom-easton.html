Temple Chayai Shalom

Bar & Bat Mitzvah Photography for South Shore Families

Temple Chayai Shalom in Easton is a warm, close-knit congregation that serves Jewish families across the South Shore. It is the kind of synagogue where everybody knows everybody — and that familiarity makes for photographs filled with genuine connection. When I photograph a B'nai Mitzvah here, I am not just capturing a ceremony; I am capturing a community celebrating one of its own.

[ Photo: Ceremony at Temple Chayai Shalom, Easton ]

The sanctuary has an intimate quality that I appreciate as a photographer. There is nowhere to hide the emotion in a room this connected, and that works entirely in our favor. The expressions on the faces of parents, siblings, grandparents, and lifelong family friends are unguarded and real — exactly the kind of photographs that become the ones you return to again and again over the decades.

Easton and the surrounding South Shore communities — Stoughton, Sharon, Canton, Mansfield — are home to many of the families I have served over my thirty-plus years photographing B'nai Mitzvahs. Many receptions following Temple Chayai Shalom celebrations take place at venues in the area, and I am well acquainted with the timing and flow that makes these days run beautifully.

[ Photo: Candid moments at Temple Chayai Shalom ]

If your family is part of the Temple Chayai Shalom community, I would be glad to bring that same care and experience to your child's milestone.
