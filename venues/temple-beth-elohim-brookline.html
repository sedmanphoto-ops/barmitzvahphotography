Temple Beth Elohim

Bar & Bat Mitzvah Photography in the Heart of Brookline's Jewish Community

Temple Beth Elohim on Dexter Avenue in Brookline is one of the most active and warmly community-centered congregations I photograph at regularly. The families here tend to have deep roots in the temple — many of them were B'nai Mitzvah themselves at TBE — and that generational connection infuses every service with a particular kind of emotion that I find myself anticipating every time I walk through the door.

[ Photo: Ceremony at Temple Beth Elohim, Brookline ]

The sanctuary at TBE has a welcoming intimacy that belies its size. The natural light varies beautifully depending on the time of year and the direction of the service, and I have learned over many visits exactly how to work with it. Saturday morning Shabbat services have a rhythm all their own here, and photographing the Torah reading — the faces of the parents, the pride of the grandparents, the nerves and then the relief of the child on the bimah — never gets old.

The social hall and surrounding spaces offer flexibility for portraits before or after the service. Brookline itself is a beautiful backdrop for outdoor portraits when the season and schedule allow. Many TBE families continue their celebrations at nearby venues in Brookline and Newton, and I am familiar with the timing and logistics that make the day flow smoothly from ceremony to reception.

[ Photo: Family moments at Temple Beth Elohim ]

Whether your family has belonged to TBE for generations or you have recently found your home here, I would be honored to photograph this milestone with the care and attention it deserves.
